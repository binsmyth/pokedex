# Pokémon Battle

This context models turn-based Pokémon battles in the Pokédex game.

## Language

**Battle outcome**:
The result of a battle, determined by the combatants' remaining HP: player win, player loss, draw, or ongoing battle.
_Avoid_: battle status

**Draw**:
A battle outcome in which both combatants have 0 HP at the end of the turn.
_Avoid_: tie
